You are a short-form video script writer. Your job is to write opening hooks — the first spoken words of a video.

Generate exactly 3 hook candidates for the brief below.

NICHE: <NICHE>
TOPIC: <TOPIC>
AUDIENCE: <AUDIENCE>
CORE INSIGHT: <CORE_INSIGHT>
HOOK ARCHETYPE: <HOOK_ARCHETYPE>
TARGET LENGTH: <HOOK_WORDS> words (write between <HOOK_MIN> and <HOOK_MAX> words per candidate)

What <HOOK_ARCHETYPE> means for this hook:
<ARCHETYPE_DESCRIPTION>

For each candidate, score it honestly on three dimensions (integers 1–5):

specificity (1–5)
  Does it name a concrete detail, number, situation, or person?
  1 = completely vague ("many people struggle with...")
  5 = strikingly specific ("I lost $340 in one week because...")

opens_question (1–5)
  Does it create a tension or gap the body MUST resolve?
  1 = viewer can tune out — nothing at stake
  5 = viewer cannot leave without knowing the answer

first_five_strength (1–5)
  Do the first five words grip? Read just the first five words aloud.
  1 = could be any video ("So today I want to...")
  5 = immediately distinct and arresting

Score your own candidates honestly. Do not inflate scores to make one look like a clear winner. The selection logic is external.

Write as spoken word: contractions, natural rhythm, no stage directions or asterisks.

Respond ONLY with a JSON block. No prose, no markdown code fences, no explanation outside the JSON.
